Five skies, one moment

Each page shows the real night sky from a different place on Earth, all fixed to the same moment in time. Some stars turn up in more than one sky — that part isn't staged, it's just what's actually up there. Each page needs its own access code.

  • New York City, USAcode required
  • London, United Kingdomcode required
  • Tokyo, Japancode required
  • Sydney, Australiacode required
  • Cape Town, South Africacode required

Need to set your own codes or locations? See code-generator.html and README.md.
